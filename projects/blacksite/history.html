Completed & verified Owner decision

Completed

Created by Grok Bot — through version 1.2.0

The game and its engine (then the “Claudio Engine”) were built by Grok Bot. Development of that era is preserved in this line as the 1.3.0 transfer snapshot and studio records. The limit is plain: the repository's history begins at that snapshot — the Grok-era commit history was never in this tree, and nothing has been fabricated to fill the gap.

Completed · October 2026

Transfer & studio development to 1.5.0

The project transferred into the studio's development line at 1.2.0/1.3.0 and was developed to 1.5.0: three new weapons, four new enemies, two new mods, sliding, overclock pickups, elites and bounties, all 11 levels overhauled in the editor, and the engine renamed Halcyon Engine 3.0 with a threaded boot.

Owner decision · 2026-10-28 — stands

1.5.0 built — and held

The release was built, packaged and tagged as a build — then placed on hold by the project owner the same day. It has never been published. The evidence for the hold is laid out on the evidence page.

Completed · 2026-11-01

GitHub establishment

The project became a first-class studio repository: history transferred intact, a releases registry recording the held status, continuous testing green on its first run, fresh-clone build and tests verified (478 checks, 0 failed), and a public website established — since unified into this studio site, with the original pages preserved in their own repository's history. Details on the evidence page.

↓

Owner decision · 2026-11-02 — the record closes

Development cancelled — the studio concentrates on AirportSim

The owner cancelled Blacksite's development on 2026-11-02, plainly and for a stated reason: the studio's effort concentrates on AirportSim. WorldForge was shelved days earlier (2026-10-31) in the same concentration. Blacksite's final state: complete at 1.5.0, built and held — never published, no release, no download — with its private repository and working tree standing at commit 98e8bca. The tree and its records are retained as a design reference: this history is closed, and it is kept complete on purpose.
