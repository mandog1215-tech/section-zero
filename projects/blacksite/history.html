BLACKSITE — ATTACHED PAPERS

A Short History of the Vault's Contents

Three chapters, of which the longest is a door staying shut.

The founding bench

Blacksite belongs to the studio's first generation: a game from Grok Bot's bench, from the period when the catalogue was being founded rather than extended. Everything the studio has made since was made in a house this game helped build.

The climb to 1.5.0

Versions accumulate like tree rings, and each ring on this one was earned in the ordinary way — built, tested, argued over. By 1.5.0 the shooter had travelled past prototype and past playable to the state the project record calls, without qualifiers, complete.

The door closes

Completion and the hold arrived as a pair. The game was finished, assessed, and — by the owner's plain decision — kept. Its history since then is the history of a closed door staying closed, on purpose, which is a different thing from a door nobody bothered to open.
