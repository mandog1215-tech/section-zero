THE DECISION

Finished and released are two different verbs

What "held" means here

Blacksite is not unfinished, not broken, and not waiting on one more patch. It reached version 1.5.0 as a complete piece of work. Holding it is a distribution decision, made by the owner, on purpose — the studio would rather keep a good game in the vault than push it out the door before its moment.

Why show you a sealed folder?

Because the studio's whole way of working is on-the-record, and the record includes the games you cannot play yet. A vault with nothing in it is a cupboard. This one holds the studio's first finished shooter, and its existence shapes everything built since — AirportSim's standards were learned in a house where finishing things, fully, was already the norm.
