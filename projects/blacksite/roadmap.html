Current position

A complete, held game with a proper home

Blacksite's development is cancelled, by the owner's decision of 2026-11-02: the game is finished to 1.5.0, its records are in order, and no further development is planned. The cancellation was a concentration decision — the studio's effort goes to AirportSim, and WorldForge was shelved at the same time. The private repository and working tree stand at commit 98e8bca, and the tree and records are retained as a design reference. The release question below is separate and remains the owner's to direct. The story of how it got here is on the history page.

Releasing 1.5.0, or retiring the hold, is a future owner decision; no date is attached to it, and nothing on this site should be read as a countdown. Until that decision exists, the version remains staged and held.
