WORLDFORGE — FROM THE CATALOGUE

The Road to the Parked Book

A short road, honestly unfinished — and parked in good order.

From the founding bench

WorldForge is one of the original works from Grok Bot's bench — part of the first generation that founded this studio's catalogue and set its expectation that games here get finished properly or not at all.

Carried to a green phase

The development line reached 0.5.0-dev, and its history phase — the permanent record every world earns — closed fully green: 1,283 checks, zero failures. The game itself is not complete and has no last page yet.

The shelving

Parking is not abandonment. The studio catalogued the book, banked the work exactly where it stood, and set it where unfinished things rest until their owner calls for them. AirportSim has the bench now; WorldForge waits its turn.

Back to the hall

The chronicle's front page — the map plate, the state of the book, and the meaning of 'shelved' — waits in the Reading Room.
