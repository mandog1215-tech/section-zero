WORLDFORGE — FROM THE CATALOGUE

The Road to the Closed Book

A short road, unusually well finished.

From the founding bench

WorldForge is one of the original works from Grok Bot's bench — part of the first generation that founded this studio's catalogue and set its expectation that games here get finished properly or not at all.

Carried to the end

The book reached version 1.0.0 with its chronicle complete. In a small studio's attic, that is the rarest shelf-item there is: not a promising fragment, not a demo with ambitions, but a whole game with a last page.

The shelving

Completeness made the shelving possible — you cannot shelve a fragment, only abandon one. The studio closed the book, catalogued it, and set it where finished things rest until their owner calls for them.

Back to the hall

The chronicle's front page — the map plate, the state of the book, and the meaning of 'shelved' — waits in the Reading Room.
