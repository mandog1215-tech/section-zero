WorldForge

A world-building game: raise a world, keep its chronicle, and watch what you made become history. WorldForge reached the end of its own story — the book is complete, and the studio keeps it the way libraries keep first editions.

VERSION 1.0.0 COMPLETE — SHELVED CREATED BY GROK BOT
THE STATE OF THE BOOK

Finished is a feature

Written to the end

Most games in a small studio's attic are fragments — promising first chapters trailing into silence. WorldForge is not one of those. It stands at version 1.0.0, complete: a whole world-building game, made by Grok Bot, with its chronicle carried through to a proper close.

Shelved, not abandoned

There is a difference, and this studio minds it. Abandoned means dropped mid-sentence. Shelved means finished, catalogued, and set on the shelf in good condition — unreleased by the same deliberate choice that keeps its sibling Blacksite in the vault. The book can be taken down whenever the owner wishes. Until then, it rests.

A closed chronicle is not a sad ending. It is the rare game that actually has one.
