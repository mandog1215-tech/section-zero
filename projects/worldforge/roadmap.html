The Shelf, For Now

WorldForge is parked, not finished. Its road resumes only when the owner calls it back.

Where the road starts

From banked ground: a 0.5.0-dev line whose history phase closed fully green, and a full-road plan on file — observatory work first, then the living world, trade, the hearth, the deluge and bedrock. None of it is active while AirportSim holds the bench.

What the road is

The studio works one game at a time, and the one game is AirportSim. WorldForge's road stays exactly where it was banked until the owner says otherwise.
