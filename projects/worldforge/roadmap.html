The Shelf, and What Could Leave It

A roadmap with no miles on it — only a shelf, kept properly, and a door that opens from one side.

What shelving commits us to

Custody, mostly: the game kept whole and accounted for, its records straight, its state described honestly wherever the studio mentions it. A finished book does not go stale the way a half-built game does; the shelf asks little, and the studio pays it gladly.

What could change it

The owner's word — a revival, a release, a new chapter ordered from the chair that owns all such calls. Nothing in the game's condition argues against it, and nothing on the calendar argues for it. The book waits the way books do: patiently, and in full.
