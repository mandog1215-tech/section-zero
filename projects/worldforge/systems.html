WORLDFORGE — FROM THE CATALOGUE

How a World Gets Forged

The Reading Room presents this game the way the studio keeps it: as a bound thing — a world on one side of the covers, and its chronicle on the other.

Building, as authorship

WorldForge is a world-building game: the player's material is a world itself, and the work is the making of it. Where most of the studio's games are about running something — an airport, a voyage — this one is about bringing something into being and answering for what it becomes.

The chronicle habit

The studio's taste for kept records shows here in its purest form. A forged world earns a chronicle; the chronicle is kept; the kept chronicle is, in the end, the monument. It is no accident that the studio's living game now gives its airlines memories of their own — the habit was already in the house.

Back to the hall

The chronicle's front page — the map plate, the state of the book, and the meaning of 'shelved' — waits in the Reading Room.
