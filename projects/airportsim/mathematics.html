AIRPORTSIM — FIELD NOTES

The Sums Under the Tarmac

AirportSim looks like a cartoon and computes like an audit. Three kinds of arithmetic hold the whole field up.

Deterministic chance

Every world is dealt from a seed, and the dealing is guarded: produce the same seed twice and the same airport wakes up, traffic and all. That is what makes a bug re-photographable and a fix provable — the scene of the crime can be restaged exactly, as many times as the argument requires.

Measured geometry

Stands, fuselages and terminal walls are rectangles with opinions, and the great repair was geometry rather than taste: 2,252 stands repositioned across 26 layouts, and at Detroit's stand A40 the clearance at a parked nose went from 16.9 metres to 39.7. Numbers first; the paint followed.

The one-sentence law

Each airline decision must be explainable in a single plain sentence drawn from its actual causes — the ledger, the fees, the rival's offer. A decision the arithmetic cannot narrate about itself is treated as a defect in the arithmetic, and it does not ship.
