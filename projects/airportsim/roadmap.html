AIRPORTSIM — FIELD NOTES

The Road to the Finished Article

AirportSim's remaining distance: what already stands behind it, what is under construction now, and the gate at the end of the road.

Already behind us

The roster stands at 58 aircraft types wearing their airlines' colours. The great stand repair and the collision programme are closed — measured, photographed and merged. Ground markings are painted to real-world dimensions, the Detroit streak defect was found and fixed, and the static ground now renders from baked chunks, roughly twice as fast in the test frame.

Under construction now

One thing: the platform move. The game is being ported fully to Windows and the Linux build is scrapped — design first, then the build. The 7.0 'Latitude' closed test copy was delivered on 17 November 2026 and is in closed testing; the deeper roles and systems follow the port. The brand-new interface, designed from a blank page with no inherited pieces, is drawn but not yet built.

The foundation

The SDL2-to-SDL3 change is behind the game: AirportSim now stands on SDL3, with its simulation kept separate from its drawing so whole airports can be tested headlessly.

The final gate

The road ends at a handover: a finished test copy, inspected, played long and honestly, and reported on in full — and then judged. That last step is not delegated, scheduled around, or hurried.

Back to the hall

The airfield itself — diagram, airlines, photographs and one small landing strip — waits on the main page of this hall.
