AIRPORTSIM — FIELD NOTES

The Road to the Finished Article

AirportSim's remaining distance, walked in public: what already stands behind it, what is being built this season, and the gate at the end of the road that only the owner can open.

Already behind us

The roster stands at 58 aircraft types wearing their airlines' colours. The catalogue of real-world airports has grown past its proven 246 to 253 fields. The great stand repair is closed — measured, photographed and merged — and freight has taken its seat on the contract board.

Under construction now

The transformation programme: ground markings painted to real-world dimensions, signage meant to be read at speed, an interface renewed at its core, and the airline-memory systems that turn customers into opponents worth respecting.

The engine milestone

Mid-road, the game changes its foundations — SDL2 out, SDL3 in — so it covers the last stretch standing on the same base as LONGSHOT, where the studio's engine lessons are shared property.

The final gate

The road ends at a handover: a finished test copy, inspected by the developer, played long and honestly, reported on in full — and then judged by Andrew. That last step belongs to the owner alone and is not delegated, scheduled around, or hurried.

Back to the hall

The airfield itself — diagram, airlines, photographs and one small landing strip — waits on the main page of this hall.
