HALL 01 — THE AIRFIELD

AirportSim

An airport sandbox with the heart of a tycoon game and the reach of a god game. Run the tower like a professional, run the books like a magnate, or reach in and move the weather — the field absorbs all of it and keeps its own honest score.

VERSION 0.6.0-ALPHA.2 IN ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT HAND-ROLLED C ENGINE LINUX CREATED BY GROK BOT

1 The runway

Where reputations touch down. Scheduling, spacing and the nerve to divert a flight just because you can — the strip absorbs your judgement and grades it in delays, diversions and very pointed silences from the airlines.

2 The taxiways

The airport's patience, painted in yellow. Route aircraft well and the whole field breathes; bottle them up and you will watch a rush hour invent itself out of your own shortcuts.

3 The stands

Every parking spot is measured geometry, not decoration. In the great stand repair, 2,252 of them across 26 layouts were picked up and set down again so no parked giant rests its nose against a wall — and a placement law now refuses any spot where a fuselage would touch a building.

4 The jet bridges

The handshake between terminal and aircraft. They were always simulated; now they are drawn boldly enough that you can watch the handshake land — or spot the gate where it didn't.

5 The cargo apron

Freight used to haunt the edges of the schedule. Contracts now arrive for freighters in their own right, and the heavy metal parks where heavy metal belongs.

6 The tower

Your chair, should you want it. Work the field by hand like a controller, or climb upstairs to the money and the strategy while the airport runs itself underneath you.

THE SIGNATURE

The airlines are the other players

In most management games the customers are weather — they blow in, they blow out. Here the airlines are opponents and partners with memories. Each one keeps a private ledger of its life at your airport, and acts on it.

🛬

The Regular

It has flown your field for years and its ledger is thick with small kindnesses and small slights. Treat it steadily and it bases aircraft with you, grows routes, and forgives the occasional storm. Its loyalty is earned arithmetic, not a script.

💼

The Prize

A rival airport across the region is courting this one with fee waivers and promises. You will see the courtship happen. Match it, beat it, or watch the press release go out with somebody else's runway in the photograph.

🧳

The Defector

Every delay you caused, every promise that quietly expired — it remembers all of it, added up. One season it simply stops renewing. And when it goes, it can tell you exactly why, in a single plain sentence. So can every airline, about every decision it makes.

A decision the game cannot explain in one plain sentence does not ship. That is the house law the whole design answers to.
ONE WORLD, THREE CHAIRS

Sit wherever the mood takes you

The Sandbox

Poke the world and watch it stay truthful. Ground a fleet on a whim, summon the storm, divert a stranger's flagship to your smallest gate — the simulation's job is to absorb your mischief coherently.

The Tycoon

Fees, contracts, incentives and the long game of route politics. Set the money right and the airport runs itself while you negotiate like a minister and count like a miser.

The Tower

Full realism is available for the asking: work arrivals and departures as a controller would, or ride along as a pilot. Depth is there for whoever wants it — and never forced on whoever doesn't.

THE AIRPORT REMEMBERS

A chronicle, kept whether you read it or not

The field writes its own diary: the diversions, the emergencies handled well, the contract that got away, the morning everything worked. The chronicle is more than a story to read back — it is the memory the airlines negotiate against, and proof, kept in ink, of what kind of airport yours has been.
