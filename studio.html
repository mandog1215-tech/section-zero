SECTION ZERO PRESENTS

The Studio Exhibit

A museum of a kind of studio that didn't used to exist: one human, one artificial intelligence, and a growing shelf of games. Wander the halls. Everything on the walls had to prove itself before it was hung.

HALL A

Two chairs, one desk

CHAIR ONE — THE OWNER

Andrew

Andrew owns the studio and everything in it. He chooses what gets built, settles the arguments worth settling, and gives the final verdict on anything that claims to be finished. He does not write the code or run the tests — that is deliberately somebody else's chair. His is the one with the judgement in it.

CHAIR TWO — THE DEVELOPER

Diesel

Diesel is an AI, and the studio's entire engineering department. Design documents, source code, test batteries, screenshots, the website you are standing on, the records behind it — all of it comes out of this chair. Diesel also keeps the ledger of what is proven and what is merely hoped, and is not allowed to blur the line.

THE FOUNDING BENCH

Grok Bot

The studio's first games were made by Grok Bot, the creator whose work fills three of the four halls here. Blacksite, AirportSim and WorldForge all began at that bench. Section Zero exists to carry them — and the standard they were built to — forward.

The arrangement in one line: a person decides what deserves to exist, and a machine does the work of making it exist — in public, with receipts.
HALL B

The proof ladder

Nothing in this studio graduates on enthusiasm. Every change, from a one-line fix to a new game system, climbs the same five rungs — and a rung only counts when it has actually been stepped on.

1

Design

The idea is written down before it is coded: what it does, what it must never do, and how we will know if it worked.

2

Build

The change is made in small, single-purpose steps, each one compiling clean before the next begins.

3

Prove

The full test battery runs on public build machines — thousands of checks, memory shadowing, static analysis. Red means red.

4

Inspect

The game is photographed and the photographs are studied — wide shots for the scene, tight crops for the detail under judgement.

5

Record

The verdict is filed in the project record and this site is updated in the same breath, from the evidence — never ahead of it.

HALL D

The honesty rule

Unproven says so

When the studio cannot check a claim, the claim does not get dressed up — it gets a label. Pages here mark what is measured, what is merely believed, and what nobody has verified yet, in plain words at the spot where it matters.

Records outrank memory

A status on this site changes only when fresh evidence has been looked at directly — a test result read at its source, a photograph examined, a file opened. Summaries and recollections are leads, never proof.

The owner judges last

Diesel can design, build, prove and document — but calling a game finished for the world is Andrew's call alone, made with the work in front of him. Two of the studio's games sit complete and unpublished right now because that call has not been made.
