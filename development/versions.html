DEVELOPMENT — THE RECORD ROOM

The Version Ledger

A version number is a claim about state, so this studio keeps them in a ledger like everything else. Four worlds, four numbers, four quite different meanings.

AirportSim — 0.6.0-alpha.2

The working identity of the studio's living game: mid-transformation, in active development, wearing its alpha honestly. Its finished identity will be the owner's to bestow at the handover.

Blacksite — 1.5.0

A complete game under a hold. The number is final in every sense except the public one: no release has been called, so 1.5.0 is where the shooter rests, whole.

WorldForge — 1.0.0

The rarest entry in the ledger: a game that reached its round number and stopped because it was done. Shelved complete; the number needs no sequel to justify it.

LONGSHOT — awaiting its number

The voyage was deferred before any public identity was set. Its bench records stand at twelve published values reproduced of twelve — the number it eventually wears will be assigned when the owner calls the resumption.
