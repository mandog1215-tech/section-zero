The Version Ledger

A version number is a claim about state, so this studio keeps them in a ledger like everything else. Three worlds, three numbers, three quite different meanings.

AirportSim — 0.6.0-alpha.2

The working identity of the studio's one active game: mid-overhaul, with markings and collision closed, the ground bake merged, and a brand-new interface being built and proven slice by slice. Its finished identity remains the owner's to bestow at the handover.

Blacksite — 1.5.0

An unfinished game under a closed file. No release has been called and none is coming: 1.5.0 is simply where the shooter stopped, and the stall is indefinite.

WorldForge — 0.5.0-dev

The development line of an unfinished, parked game. A 1.0.0 candidate is held in the project records, but the owner has not called WorldForge finished and nothing has been released; the number the finished game will wear is the owner's to say.
