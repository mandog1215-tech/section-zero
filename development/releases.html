DEVELOPMENT — THE RECORD ROOM

The Release Ledger

Two lists live in this drawer, and the studio is careful never to let them touch: what is finished, and what has been released. They are not the same list. At this studio, so far, they barely overlap at all.

Released to the public

Nothing — yet, and by choice rather than by accident. As this ledger is written, the public column is empty because the owner has not called a release, and releases here are called, never drifted into.

Finished, and held

Blacksite, complete at 1.5.0, rests in the vault. WorldForge, complete at 1.0.0, rests on the shelf. Two whole games, done, unpublished — the clearest possible proof that in this house, finishing and releasing are separate crafts with separate calendars.

Still in the workshop

AirportSim is mid-transformation under its working identity, and LONGSHOT is parked in good order awaiting the owner's word to resume. Neither appears in either column above, and the ledger is comfortable saying so.

The rule of the drawer

A version number in a drawer is not a launch, a tag is not a publication, and 'basically done' is not a state this ledger recognises. When the first release is called, it will be entered here in the same plain hand as everything else.

Back to the hall

The record room's front desk keeps the latest entry, the three drawers, and the specimen case.
